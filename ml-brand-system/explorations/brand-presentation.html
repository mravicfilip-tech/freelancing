02 The idea

Three bars rise. One holds them up.

A lever moves what muscle can't. The mark shows the mechanic in four strokes: a business getting bigger, and the lever that carries it.

  1. Bars

    A business getting bigger

    Three bars climb in equal steps, read left to right. The same rising bars the old logo opened with.

  2. Lever

    What carries the growth

    The fourth stroke leans in and props up the tallest bar. That is the lever. It does the work so the owner doesn't have to.

  3. Peak

    Maximum

    Together they form a peak. The highest point sits on the right: the after, not the before.

→

A homage, not a restart. The rising bars, the rounded stroke ends and the wide lettering all come from the logo clients already know. The gradient and gloss are gone.

Two faces, three jobs.

06 Motion

Rise. Lean. Settle.

The mark moves the way the idea works. The bars rise first, then the lever leans in and takes the weight, with a small overshoot as it lands.
