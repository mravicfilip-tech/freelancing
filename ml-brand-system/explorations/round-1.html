Same idea. Simpler mark. Flat color. New type.

Three asks: replace the five-bar symbol, drop the Ignition gradient for flat color, and move from Montserrat and Michroma to a modern grotesk. Five options for each. The idea underneath stays put: a lever moves what muscle can't, and everything rises from bottom-left to top-right.

No gradients anywhere. One accent per palette, used on the mark, the primary button and one phrase per headline. Contrast figures are computed from the hex values: body text and button labels need 4.5:1, the mark needs 3:1.

Each option is one grotesk family doing everything: wordmark, headlines, body and tracked labels. That retires Michroma and Montserrat together. All five are free on Google Fonts; the paid face with a similar feel is listed in case the budget allows.
