04

Sweep, told as a story

The mark is not a picture of a lever. It is the trace a lever leaves when it moves. That is why it can carry a story and a motion at the same time.

Why it works, as a designer

One idea
A lever turns 60 degrees. The short arm sweeps a small wedge, the long arm sweeps a large one. Less effort, more scale, drawn exactly rather than illustrated.
The ratio
The arms are 1 to 3. The small wedge is 20 units, the large one 60. The same ratio sets clear space and the layout grid, so the whole system shares one number.
The ground line
Both wedges start on one flat edge. That edge is the owner's hours. It does not move. Your business grows. Your hours don't.
Why it is premium
Two shapes, one angle, one pivot. Circles and straight lines only. No outline, no effect, nothing to simplify later.
Why it scales
A solid shape with no thin strokes. The arcs are circular, so the mark settles into round avatars instead of fighting them.
Why it moves
Its logo animation is the idea itself: the arm swings and the wedges fill in. It works as a one-second sting at the end of every video.
What could go wrong
Seen alone, the big wedge can read as a pie chart. The counter-wedge below the line is what stops that, so it is never dropped. Under 20px the small wedge thickens slightly in a separate favicon cut.
