Wedge.

Round 2 was diagrams. This round goes deeper on one idea instead of wider on five, drawn with the same care as a typeface. Below it: what I killed on the way here, and why.

The idea

01

The wedge is the oldest machine there is. Drive it in, and a small push splits what muscle can't. Two posts and a wedge also happen to spell M.

Why it works

02

It is a real machine, not a picture of one

A wedge is a lever family member, the same physics the brand is named after. No arrows, no charts, no rising lines. The idea survives without the tagline next to it.

The letter comes for free

Nobody has to be told it is an M. Two verticals and a V is how the letter is built. The machine and the monogram are the same three shapes.

One thing is out of line, on purpose

The posts sit on the baseline and share a cap height. The wedge alone rises above them. That single break is the force in the mark, and the eye goes straight to it.

The gaps do the drawing

The two gaps taper from 4 units at the top to 21 at the tip. That taper is what makes it read as M. With parallel gaps the negative space takes over and it reads as V. I tested it, it is in the killed sketches.

Three solid parts, nothing thin

No strokes, no outlines, no detail smaller than the top gap. It holds at 16px and sits in a circle avatar with even margins, because its footprint is almost square.

It animates as the story

The wedge drops in, the posts give a little, everything settles. One second. That is the video sting, and it tells the idea without a voiceover.

Construction

03
Grid
96 by 96 units, drawn on a 4-unit grid.
Posts
17 wide, 60 tall, flat tops at y 24. Outer edges at 10 and 86.
Wedge
38 wide at the top, set 12 units above the posts. The tip stops at y 70, 14 short of the baseline: driven, not finished.
Gaps
5.9 at the post tops, 21 at the tip. Always tapered, never parallel.
Clear space
One post width, 17 units, on every side.
Small sizes
Next step: a separate cut for 24px and below, with the tip blunted to 3 units and the top gaps widened to 7 so they stay open on screen.

Judging it

Where round 2 went wrong

  • I drew diagrams of the idea (a pie slice, a split circle) instead of marks. An idea is not a mark until it is drawn.
  • No construction. Knife-point tips, arbitrary coordinates, nothing optically centred.
  • I scored my own pick 27 out of 30. That was grading, not judging. The Sweep reads as a pie chart and I knew it.
  • Five shallow options instead of one deep one. You cannot judge a mark that has not been finished.

What could still go wrong with Wedge

  • At 12px or below it can read as "I▼I" or a download icon. The favicon cut helps. Below 12px, use the plain Signal square.
  • Stencil shapes can feel industrial. The Set drawing is the calm answer if that worries you.
  • A red M is a crowded space. It needs a trademark search before anything else happens.
