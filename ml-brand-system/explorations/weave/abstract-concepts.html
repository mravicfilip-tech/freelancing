Maximum Leverage · Patterns · Abstract concepts

Eight abstract directions. The mark's DNA, not the mark.

Each concept takes only the mark's rounded stroke ends, its 60° lean and the idea of one stroke leaning on another, then abstracts them. Drawn by Nano Banana Pro from the final logo image. Pick one and I will redraw it as a clean vector in Carbon, Chalk and Signal, alongside Summit, Ripple and Slabs.

Recommended

A01Bauhaus fragments

Recommended

The most complete abstraction. Round caps, half circles and slanted rounded bars, cut and rearranged like a Swiss poster. You feel the mark without seeing it, and it composes into endless layouts.

A06Signal boost

Recommended

Rounded strips at the mark's 60° angle, sliced and shifted like a signal being amplified. Pure brand DNA, very energetic, and it redraws cleanly as vectors.

A04Arc geometry

Recommended

The round stroke ends blown up into huge intersecting arcs, cut by 60° lines. Bold and modernist, a strong counterpoint to the straight-line patterns you already have.

A05Folded ribbon

Recommended

One ribbon folding back on itself at 60°, rising. The simplest and most confident of the set. Works as a single hero graphic behind a headline.
