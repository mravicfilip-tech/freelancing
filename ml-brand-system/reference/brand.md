# Brand: idea, messaging, voice

## The idea
Leverage is the whole idea. A lever moves what muscle can't. Maximum Leverage builds and runs the levers, so a local business grows without its owner working more hours. Every element of the identity comes from that mechanic.

- The bars: five bars that rise. Read left to right, they are a business getting bigger.
- The M: the peaks trace an M. The right peak sits higher than the left. The after, not the before.
- The lever line: a line drawn through the peaks. It is the rising diagonal used across the system, and the reason the gradient always runs bottom-left to top-right.

## Messaging (use verbatim)

**Tagline**
Less Effort. More Scale.

**Positioning line** (hero headline default)
Proven levers that grow your local business without you.

**Mission**
We build and run the growth levers the big companies use, from offers to AI to automated follow-up, so local business owners win more customers without working more hours.

**Vision**
A world where every local business can compete with the biggest names in its market.

## Audience
Local business owners, typically past $1M in revenue, who are the bottleneck in their own growth. They think in customers, calls, hours and payroll. They do not think in funnels, pipelines or MQLs.

## Voice rules
1. Short sentences. If it needs a comma, split it.
2. Numbers over adjectives. Say what changed and by how much.
3. Lever, then result. Name what you pull, then what moves.
4. Owner's language. Customers, calls, hours. Never funnels, pipelines or synergy.
5. No corporate verbs (drive, leverage as a verb, empower, unlock). No AI-sounding phrases.
6. No em dashes. Use a period or a comma.
7. One orange phrase per headline at most, and it carries the payoff ("without you", "who answered?").

## Headline patterns that fit the voice
- The loss you did not see: "Somebody called you last night. Who answered?"
- Lever, then result: "One follow-up sequence. 31% more booked consults."
- The owner's trade: "Your business grows. Your hours don't."
- Binary: "Answer every call. Or keep paying for the ones you missed."

## Words to avoid
Leverage as a verb, synergy, empower, unlock, elevate, journey, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer, robust, holistic, best-in-class.
