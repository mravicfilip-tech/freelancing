# Page recipes

Each recipe is the section order, top to bottom. Start from the matching template and cut what you do not need. Never add a section that is not on the list without a reason the owner would understand.

## Home / main landing page (`templates/landing.html`)
1. Nav with one primary button.
2. Hero, two column, dark. Positioning line as H1. Mission sentence as lead. Primary and ghost buttons. Glowing icon on the right.
3. Proof strip (light): three real numbers, or logos of businesses served. Skip if there are none.
4. The levers (dark): three or four numbered lever cards. Each names the lever and the result.
5. How it works (light): three steps, numbered in Michroma.
6. Testimonial (dark): one quote, full width, narrow container.
7. Offer or CTA band (Ignition) with the owner's-trade headline.
8. FAQ (light), five questions max.
9. Footer.

## Webinar registration (`templates/webinar-registration.html`)
1. Minimal nav: lockup only, no links.
2. Centered hero, dark: label with the event type, headline that names the loss or the result, one-sentence promise, event details strip.
3. Two-column block: left, "what you'll get" as a check list (three to five items) and the presenter; right, the registration form card. Form stays visible above the fold on desktop.
4. One testimonial or one number.
5. Repeat CTA band at the bottom pointing back to the form.
6. Footer with fine print.

## Webinar replay / thank-you (`templates/thank-you.html`)
1. Lockup only.
2. Label "You're in" or "Replay", H2 with the next step, short lead.
3. Video frame (or calendar add buttons when it is a confirmation).
4. Three-step "what happens next".
5. Single CTA band: book a call.
6. Footer.

## Sales / offer page
1. Nav, lockup only.
2. Centered hero with the loss headline and one orange phrase.
3. The problem (light): three short paragraphs in owner's language.
4. The levers (dark): numbered.
5. Proof (light): numbers, then one quote.
6. Offer card, featured, with a check list and one price. Guarantee under it as fine print.
7. FAQ.
8. Final CTA band.
9. Footer.

## About
1. Nav.
2. Hero: "Leverage is the whole idea." H1 with the lever line diagram or glowing icon.
3. The idea: three columns (bars, M, lever line) from `reference/brand.md`.
4. Mission and vision as two cards.
5. Team or presenter, one column each, no stock photos.
6. CTA band. Footer.

## Email header and signature
- Header: Night background, lockup `--md`, tagline label beneath in Silver.
- Signature: icon in a navy 56px tile, three lines of text, tagline as a tracked caps label in muted gray.

## Slides
- Title slide: Night with glow, wordmark, title in Montserrat 800, tagline label.
- Content slides: Off-white with navy text, section numeral in Michroma Flame top-right.
