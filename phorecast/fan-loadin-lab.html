Fan load-in lab

Five entrances for the “Your Funds Stay in Your Control” band. All five obey the same brief — arcs enter from the outer edges and travel inward, every line is its own staggered tween, the heading and sub-line resolve out of blur, nothing tracks the pointer. What changes between them is the mechanism: what leads, in what order, and how a line is revealed. They are not five speeds of one idea.

Each one plays once when it scrolls into view, and again on its Replay button. Answer with a number.

Open at /fan-loadin-lab.html on the dev server, or just double-click the file. Nothing here is wired into src/.

Half speed only slows playback — it does not change any variant.
