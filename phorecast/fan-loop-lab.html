Fan loop lab

Five ambient loops for the “Your Funds Stay in Your Control” band. Each one runs continuously and lands back exactly on the Figma frame at the end of every cycle. Answer with a number — the chosen one gets wired into src/components/fan/Fan.loop.ts.

These are five different ideas, not one idea at five speeds: they differ in where the light is born, which way it travels, what the tile’s light does, and whether the pills and diamonds take part. In all five the arc lines carry something and the logo tile has a light of its own.

Nothing here responds to the pointer, nothing floats or breathes, and the tile’s light is a light rather than a strobe. Open the file directly — no dev server needed.

All five A loop only runs while it is on screen
