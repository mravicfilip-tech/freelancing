Hero ground, light mode — five candidates

Real tokens, real geometry. Each candidate changes paint only: the five disc fills, their opacities and the halftone, plus — on Dawn sky — the band’s own paper and the horizon that cuts it. Positions, radii, blur and paint order are frozen, and dark is untouched. In the build these sit behind :root[data-hero-variant='N']; with no attribute the section paints exactly what it painted before.

The slide buttons are the carousel’s three real ground anchorings: slides 1 and 3 share the default, slide 2 pushes the discs hard left under the copy, slide 4 pushes them right. A candidate that only works on slide 1 has not been looked at.
