Hero ground, light mode — five candidates

Real tokens, real geometry, real DOM. These five are five different ideas, not five settings of one: structure, material, colour, a moved light source, and no light source at all. Each is free to move, reshape, unblur, switch off or blend the five discs and to use the two spare pseudo-elements on the band — but never to add an element, and never to set left on a disc, which would freeze the carousel’s three anchorings. Dark is untouched. In the build these sit behind :root[data-hero-variant='N']; with no attribute the section paints exactly what it painted before.

The slide buttons are the carousel’s three real ground anchorings: slides 1 and 3 share the default, slide 2 pushes the discs hard left under the copy, slide 4 pushes them right. A candidate that only works on slide 1 has not been looked at.

Every number printed beside a candidate was measured on the built page at 1600 with the foreground removed, so it is the colour actually behind the glyph; the market-card plates were measured with the cards mounted and their contents hidden, because 12.25px of backdrop blur flattens a dot screen or a hairline before it reaches the glass. Body text under 4.5:1 disqualifies a candidate. Dev server only: npm run dev, then localhost:5173/hero-light-lab.html.
