Hover lab

Five directions for button hovers, menu links and footer links. Each row styles all three families the same way, so choosing a row chooses a system. Mixing is fine if a row's buttons work but its links do not.

Every button shows all three variants, and each row also carries the pressed, keyboard-focus and disabled states so nothing is missing at pick time. Tab through a row to see focus. Nothing on this page tracks the pointer.

Open at /hover-lab.html on the dev server, or just double-click the file.
