Five ways the 60-second card can move

The same onboarding card — real markup, real CSS, straight out of Bento.tsx — built five times over. Each copy is wired to a different mechanism, not the same idea at five speeds. Everything is driven by GSAP; direction 05 adds a Three.js/WebGL halftone layer and degrades to a dithered CSS sheen if WebGL is unavailable.

Hover any card. Click 01, 03 and 04 to replay or fire their accent beat. Press Tab — focusing a card's Open an Account link triggers the same motion without a mouse. 01 also plays once when it scrolls into view.
