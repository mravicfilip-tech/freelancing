Slider lab

Five slide indicators with no numerals. Four marks already say how many there are and which one is lit already says where you are, so the digits were carrying nothing the shape did not.

What every one of them keeps is the clock. The hero advances by itself every seven seconds, and a control that does not show that time running out makes the carousel look like it jumps on its own.

All five are live and clickable, and pause on hover. Open at /slider-lab.html.
