Slider lab

Five redesigns of the hero's slide indicator. The problem all of them fix is the same: the hero advances by itself every seven seconds and the current control never says so, so four identical dashes leave the carousel looking like it jumps on its own.

All five are live on the real seven second clock. Click any target to jump. Hover one to pause it, the way the hero pauses on hover. Open at /slider-lab.html, or double-click the file.
