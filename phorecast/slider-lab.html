Slider lab

Five slider controls for the hero. These are controls, not indicators: each one has prev and next, a track you can click or drag, keyboard support, and real hover and pressed states. No digits.

The handle sits at slide plus progress, so it travels the rail continuously over the full cycle rather than hopping between four stops. That is what makes it worth dragging — the thing you grab is already where you expect it to be.

All five are live on the hero's seven second clock. Drag the rail, click it, use the arrows, or focus a rail and press left and right. Hovering or dragging pauses it. Open at /slider-lab.html.
