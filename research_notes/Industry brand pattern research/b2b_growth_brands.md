## 1. What patterns / graphic devices does each brand use?

### Takeaway
Consultancies use restrained, high-contrast systems where one proprietary device carries the brand: McKinsey's "Partnership Mark" line device and Bower serif, Accenture's ">" in purple, Bain's up-right arrow in a ring, BCG's "unlocking potential" motion system. Marketing and growth SaaS brands rely more on warmth and colour: HubSpot's mid-century geometric shapes, Mailchimp's Cavendish yellow plus commissioned illustration, Klaviyo's flag, Gong's burst mark in purple and pink, and Salesforce's "Atmosphere" with 3D icons and mascots. The 2025-26 refreshes (Salesforce, Semrush, Klaviyo) move toward simpler, bolder and more minimal systems framed around AI.

### Cited Findings

**McKinsey & Company (Wolff Olins, launched Feb 2019; older than 2020 but still current)**
- New identity by Wolff Olins covered the brand mark, colours, images, fonts and layouts. It launched in February 2019 — [Consultancy.asia](https://www.consultancy.asia/news/2044/mckinsey-gets-a-makeover-for-the-digital-consulting-era); [Wolff Olins](https://wolffolins.com/work/mckinsey)
- The "Partnership Mark" is a dynamic element described as "a mark in perpetual motion" that stands for the agile way McKinsey works with clients. Its "simplified, illusionary line design" is used heavily on social media — [Transform magazine](https://www.transformmagazine.net/articles/2019/mckinsey-builds-new-confident-identity-based-on-contrasts/); [BOD / Medium](https://medium.com/@bod_consulting/bright-and-bold-the-rebranding-of-mckinsey-company-ff170b64b329)
- The guiding principle is "high contrast". The new "McKinsey deep blue" sits against white, with Electric Blue and Cyan added. Type contrasts the characterful Bower serif with the clean Theinhardt sans — [Transform magazine](https://www.transformmagazine.net/articles/2019/mckinsey-builds-new-confident-identity-based-on-contrasts/); [BOD / Medium](https://medium.com/@bod_consulting/bright-and-bold-the-rebranding-of-mckinsey-company-ff170b64b329)
- Bower is a bespoke serif named after founder Marvin Bower and made with typographer Radim Pesko. McKinsey Sans also exists — [Slideworks](https://slideworks.io/resources/decoding-mckinseys-visual-identity-and-powerpoint-template)
- Data design agency Signal Noise built "a complete data visualisation package". The Laundry Room systemised a custom photographic style — [Transform magazine](https://www.transformmagazine.net/articles/2019/mckinsey-builds-new-confident-identity-based-on-contrasts/)
- Territory Studio made an identity intro film, and Valentina D'Efilippo has a data-vis case study on the rebrand — [Territory Studio](https://territorystudio.com/project/mckinsey-identity-intro/); [apostrophe.one](https://apostrophe.one/case-studies/mckinsey-co-rebrand)
- The identity won a Red Dot award — [McKinsey blog](https://www.mckinsey.com/about-us/new-at-mckinsey-blog/the-new-mckinsey-look-gets-a-red-dot); [Red Dot](https://www.red-dot.org/project/mckinsey-company-40709/)

**Accenture (2020 refresh plus the "Let there be change" campaign by Droga5)**
- In 2020 the ">" symbol, part of the logo for more than 20 years, was changed to purple. The launch came with the biggest brand campaign in a decade, "Let there be change" by Droga5, which went live on 14 Oct 2020 and tripled annual media spend to about $90M — [Accenture newsroom](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2020/change-powers-accentures-biggest-brand-move-in-a-decade); [Campaign UK](https://www.campaignlive.co.uk/article/accenture-repositions-brand-campaign-droga5-triples-media-spend/1697183)

Purple is described as standing for creativity and wisdom — [logos-world (aggregator)](https://logos-world.net/accenture-logo/); [1000logos (aggregator)](https://1000logos.net/accenture-logo/)
- Brand New also covered the identity — [Brand New](https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/archives/new_logo_and_identity_for_accenture.php) (the page was blocked, so its content was not verified)

**Bain & Company (long-standing identity, no recent rebrand found)**
- The emblem is a red ring with a solid red triangle, or arrow, pointing up and to the right. The ring's outline opens at the arrow's tip. It is read as progress/growth plus "targeting" — [1000logos (aggregator)](https://1000logos.net/bain-company-logo/)
- The colour is a single red, PMS 186 C. The logo is reported as unchanged as of 2024 — [logos-world (aggregator)](https://logos-world.net/bain-company-logo/)
- Bain has a public product design system, "ADAPT", with custom-branding guidance — [ADAPT Design System](https://designsystem.adapt.bain.com/designers/guides/custom-branding/)

**BCG (Boston Consulting Group)**

**Salesforce (refresh with Athletics, around 2025-26, for the "agentic AI" era)**

- An earlier identity refresh was done by Tolleson Studio — [Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/71275357/Salesforce-Identity-Refresh). The public brand site is [Salesforce Brand Central](https://brand.salesforce.com/). Trailhead's "kaleidoscope" logo refresh kept the logo's shape and added line art — [Trailhead / Medium](https://medium.com/trailhead/new-trailhead-flogo-2c90ea5a8866)

**Semrush (2020 rebrand, then a new identity in March 2026)**
- The 2020 rebrand brought a new logo, colours and visuals. It is framed as "an engine of your marketing, ignited by a spark — a mechanism always in motion", with "simple rounded shapes" and pastel colours — [Semrush news 2020](https://www.semrush.com/news/272313-semrush-unveils-new-logo-and-refreshed-visual-identity/); [Behance – Semrush Rebranding](https://www.behance.net/gallery/109766543/Semrush-Rebranding)

**Klaviyo (2022 new identity, then a 2025 refresh by Motto)**

- In 2025 Motto delivered a refreshed vision, voice and visual system to "reclaim boldness and infuse more humanity", framing Klaviyo as "a visionary catalyst for transformational marketing" — [Brand Archive](https://brandarchive.substack.com/p/rebrand-klayvio-2025) (summary only)

**Gong (Moving Brands, 2021-22)**

- The mark combines Celebration (a burst of energy), Conversation (a speech bubble) and Individuality (a unique G shape). It contains a subliminal "G" and many points that suggest conversation — [Moving Brands](https://www.movingbrands.com/work/Gong)
- The palette is bright purple with pink, moderated by greys. The wordmark uses heavy, tightly tracked block forms, set in BN Axel. Bruno the mascot was kept. **Google Ads / Think with Google**

- Facebook became Meta in October 2021. The symbol is a continuous blue Möbius or infinity loop that reads as an "M". It is drawn as a single line, comes in 2D and 3D, and is designed to "live in motion and 3D", with the ability to rotate and twist — [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2021/11/03/meta-facebook-rebrand-infinity-loop/); [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/design/facebook-rebrand-meta-infinity-logo-10-31-2021/)

**Attio**
- Attio did a full rebrand early on to appeal to a "builder" audience that uses tools like Notion, Figma and Slack — [How They Grow](https://www.howtheygrow.co/p/how-attio-grows)

### Inferences
- Consultancies (McKinsey, Accenture, Bain) put their distinctiveness in one ownable mark plus a proprietary serif or high-contrast palette, and keep illustration to a minimum. Expertise is shown through restraint, bespoke type and bespoke data visualisation.
- Growth SaaS (HubSpot, Mailchimp, Gong, Klaviyo) used warmth (mid-century shapes, commissioned illustration, mascots, documentary photography) to stand out against "sterile blue" competitors. By 2025-26, several (Salesforce, Semrush, Klaviyo) were tightening and simplifying around AI positioning.

## 2. How is the logo's own symbol extended into a graphic system (symbol-as-frame, arrow, crop)?

### Takeaway
The clearest documented examples of a symbol becoming a system are McKinsey's Partnership Mark (a line device "in perpetual motion" used as a moving, illusionary graphic), Meta's single-line Möbius (built to animate, twist and render in 3D), Klaviyo's flag-as-exponent (a growth metaphor placed like a superscript), Accenture's ">" (a directional "better/forward" symbol) and Bain's arrow-breaking-the-ring. Documented evidence of symbol-as-frame or symbol-as-crop was not found for any of these brands.

### Cited Findings
- McKinsey: the Partnership Mark is a dynamic line device, separate from the wordmark, "in perpetual motion", used across social media as an "illusionary line design" — [Transform magazine](https://www.transformmagazine.net/articles/2019/mckinsey-builds-new-confident-identity-based-on-contrasts/); [BOD / Medium](https://medium.com/@bod_consulting/bright-and-bold-the-rebranding-of-mckinsey-company-ff170b64b329)
- Meta: the logo is drawn "as an infinite loop using a single line that can rotate and twist" and is designed to "live in motion and 3D" — [Dezeen](https://www.dezeen.com/2021/11/03/meta-facebook-rebrand-infinity-loop/)
- Klaviyo: the flag is placed as an exponent to signal exponential growth, and it also stands for "guidance" and "success in speed" — [Brandfetch](https://brandfetch.com/blog/klaviyo-new-logo-and-brand); [Klaviyo newsroom](https://www.klaviyo.com/newsroom/klaviyo-rebrand)
- Accenture: the ">" is a directional, forward-moving sign, recoloured purple in 2020 — [Accenture newsroom](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2020/change-powers-accentures-biggest-brand-move-in-a-decade); [logos-world (aggregator)](https://logos-world.net/accenture-logo/)
- Bain: an up-right arrow breaks through an open ring, read as growth plus targeting — [1000logos (aggregator)](https://1000logos.net/bain-company-logo/)
- Semrush: the "fireball"/spark is framed as the igniter of a "mechanism always in motion" and was streamlined in 2026 — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260312283554/en/Semrush-Unveils-New-Brand-Identity-to-Command-the-AI-Search-Era)
- Gong: the mark packs three meanings (a burst, a speech bubble, a "G"). Its many points suggest conversation, which gives a radiating form that can be extended — [Moving Brands](https://www.movingbrands.com/work/Gong)
- Google Ads: the "A" is built from rounded strokes and a dot, which fits Google's modular product-icon logic — [1000logos (aggregator)](https://1000logos.net/google-adwords-logo/); [Google Design](https://design.google/library/evolving-google-identity)
- HubSpot: the sprocket sits inside the wordmark as the "o". The 2022 system added mid-century geometric shapes — [Canny Creative (aggregator)](https://www.canny-creative.com/atlas/brand/hubspot/); [Aubrey Zych](https://www.aubreyzych.com/work/hubspotrebrand)

### Inferences
- Directional glyphs recur: ">", an up-right arrow, a flag or exponent, a spark. They let a static mark stand for "growth" without charts, and they are natural candidates for arrow, pointer or superscript devices in layouts.
- Line-based marks (McKinsey, Meta) extend most easily into motion and pattern. Solid, pictorial marks (Freddie, Astro, Bruno) extend as characters rather than geometry.
- HubSpot's shape library appears to be conceptually related to the sprocket (circles and spokes), but no source confirms the shapes are derived from it. See Gaps.

### Gaps
- No verified source shows HubSpot shapes being derived from the sprocket (the brief's premise), the Accenture ">" used as a crop, frame or pattern in layouts, or the Klaviyo flag used as a layout device beyond the logo. These are plausible but unconfirmed. Confirm against brand.hubspot.com, Accenture brand pages or Brand New imagery.

## 3. How do they visualise growth and data without stock charts?

### Takeaway
The documented approaches are bespoke data-vis systems (McKinsey with Signal Noise), metaphor marks (flag-as-exponent, spark/engine, arrow-through-ring, ">"), abstract "scientific" digital art (BCG), 3D icons and atmospheric fields (Salesforce 2025-26), and human documentary imagery showing customer success rather than numbers (Klaviyo).

### Cited Findings
- McKinsey commissioned "a complete data visualisation package" from Signal Noise "to enable clients to see what matters most" — [Transform magazine](https://www.transformmagazine.net/articles/2019/mckinsey-builds-new-confident-identity-based-on-contrasts/); [BOD / Medium](https://medium.com/@bod_consulting/bright-and-bold-the-rebranding-of-mckinsey-company-ff170b64b329)
- BCG uses "science-y, digital-looking" art in azure, green and yellow for backgrounds and slides, with green associated with growth — [Consulting.us](https://www.consulting.us/news/1367/boston-consulting-group-rebrands-to-tech-y-logo-drops-the)
- Klaviyo shows growth through the flag or exponent and through warm, documentary customer imagery — [Brandfetch](https://brandfetch.com/blog/klaviyo-new-logo-and-brand); [Klaviyo newsroom](https://www.klaviyo.com/newsroom/klaviyo-rebrand)
- Salesforce uses "Atmosphere", a 3D icon and illustration system, and characters — [The Brand Identity](https://the-brandidentity.com/project/athletics-updates-salesforces-brand-to-mark-its-move-into-agentic-ai)
- Semrush uses an engine/spark metaphor with rounded shapes and pastels (2020), and an "intelligence engine" story with a new palette and type (2026) — [Semrush 2020](https://www.semrush.com/news/272313-semrush-unveils-new-logo-and-refreshed-visual-identity/); [Business Wire 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260312283554/en/Semrush-Unveils-New-Brand-Identity-to-Command-the-AI-Search-Era)
- Mailchimp uses expressive commissioned illustration in place of data imagery — [Design Week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/issues/1-7-october-2018/mailchimp-rebrand-aims-to-unify-bran/)
- Similarweb mixes photography and illustration — [Similarweb blog](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/updates/announcements/new-similarweb/)

### Inferences
- There are two routes. (a) The consultancy route treats data vis as an owned craft (a bespoke chart language, high contrast, a serif for authority). (b) The SaaS route replaces charts with metaphor (flags, sparks, arrows), characters, or customer photography.

### Gaps
- No sourced detail was found on Semrush's or Similarweb's in-brand data-visualisation language (chart styles, colour ramps), or on Think with Google's data-story visual style.

## 4. Rebrand rationale from case studies

### Takeaway
The recurring reasons are scaling from startup to enterprise (Gong, Klaviyo), moving from traditional to digital or tech positioning (McKinsey, BCG, Accenture), standing out from "sterile blue" SaaS (HubSpot), and, in 2025-26, AI positioning (Salesforce's agentic enterprise, Semrush's AI search).

### Cited Findings
- McKinsey: a makeover "for the digital consulting era", built on high contrast to "cut through the visual noise" — [Consultancy.asia](https://www.consultancy.asia/news/2044/mckinsey-gets-a-makeover-for-the-digital-consulting-era); [BOD / Medium](https://medium.com/@bod_consulting/bright-and-bold-the-rebranding-of-mckinsey-company-ff170b64b329)
- BCG: to reflect a tech-informed offering, "less formal management consultancy-like" — [Consulting.us](https://www.consulting.us/news/1367/boston-consulting-group-rebrands-to-tech-y-logo-drops-the)
- Accenture: "Change" as the brand idea — "Let there be change" — [Accenture newsroom](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2020/change-powers-accentures-biggest-brand-move-in-a-decade)
- HubSpot: to be human and joyful against sterile blue corporate competitors — [Aubrey Zych](https://www.aubreyzych.com/work/hubspotrebrand)
- Gong: exponential growth and a future as a global enterprise, shedding its "startup skin" while keeping energy and fun — [Gong blog](https://www.gong.io/blog/introducing-gongs-new-visual-identity); [Gong press](https://www.gong.io/press/gong-unveils-updated-brand-as-enterprise-momentum-continues)
- Klaviyo 2022: rapid growth (headcount doubled in 2021, a Series D, customers generating $28B+ revenue) and "ownership" as the core idea. The 2025 refresh aimed to reclaim boldness and humanity — [Klaviyo newsroom](https://www.klaviyo.com/newsroom/klaviyo-rebrand); [Brand Archive](https://brandarchive.substack.com/p/rebrand-klayvio-2025)
- Salesforce: marking the move into agentic AI and reducing the brand to its essentials — [The Brand Identity](https://the-brandidentity.com/project/athletics-updates-salesforces-brand-to-mark-its-move-into-agentic-ai)
- Semrush 2026: moving from "search toolset" to "unified intelligence engine" for AI search — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260312283554/en/Semrush-Unveils-New-Brand-Identity-to-Command-the-AI-Search-Era)
- Mailchimp 2018: moving from email tool to broader marketing platform, with its first real repositioning — [Creative Review](https://www.creativereview.co.uk/mailchimp-rebrand-by-collins/)
- Meta 2021: "meta" means "beyond", reflecting the belief that "there is always more to build" — [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/design/facebook-rebrand-meta-infinity-logo-10-31-2021/)
- Attio: a rebrand aimed at the builder audience — [How They Grow](https://www.howtheygrow.co/p/how-attio-grows)

### Inferences
- The 2025-26 wave (Salesforce, Semrush, Klaviyo) is about simplifying and adding boldness for AI-era positioning. This suggests the current mood favours fewer, stronger core elements over large illustration libraries.

### Gaps
- No sourced rebrand rationale was found for Bain (no recent rebrand), Similarweb (date and agency unverified), Google Ads beyond the 2018 name consolidation, or Mailchimp after Intuit (2021+). Whether Intuit changed the Mailchimp visual system was not verified.
