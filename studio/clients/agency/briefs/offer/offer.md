## Who it is for

Funded tech teams from seed to Series B, in SaaS, AI, fintech and web3, whose product and site still look like
the first version their developers built, with a raise or a launch coming.

## Transformation summary

> We help funded tech teams whose product still looks like the first version their developers built walk into
> their raise or launch looking like the company they have become, through one team that designs and codes the
> brand, the site and the product.

## The argument behind it

The logic chain. Accepted by Filip, untested with buyers. It gets locked after 5 to 10 buyer conversations
where it lands, and before any website copy is written.

| Stage | The argument |
|---|---|
| Pain | We raised, but our product and site still look like the MVP our developers built, and the next raise or launch is when people judge it |
| Step 1 | A first version is built to prove the product works, not to earn trust. Patching it one screen at a time never changes the impression the whole thing makes |
| Step 2 | One consistent version of the company, the brand, the site and the product together, that looks like where the company is now and is live before the date that matters |
| Step 3 | Your developers belong on the roadmap, not rebuilding mockups. So the redesign has to arrive as working code, built by the people who designed it, on a fixed scope and date |
| Conclusion | One team that designs and codes your brand, site and product for a fixed published price, live before your raise or launch, with tracking that shows what changed |

## Why a buyer should believe us

We have no case study in this market yet, and the offer is built to make that easy to check. The price, the
scope and the launch date are fixed and written down before work starts. The day 30 numbers show what changed.
The same team designs and codes, so nothing is lost between a designer and the client's developers.
