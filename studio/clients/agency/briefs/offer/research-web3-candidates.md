Compiled 2026-10-17. Desktop first, no site design judged here. Every figure below comes from a page I read or a search summary, and anything I could not confirm says unknown.

## Read this first

- No project has an exact TGE date. Five have a month or quarter stated by a source dated inside the last 90 days (rows 1, 2, 7, 9, 10). The rest rely on older or calendar-only sources, flagged per row.
- "Q4 2026" can land before 2026-11-15, so rows 1, 2 and 7 may fall outside the window. Row 7 may already be live.
- Several calendar pages were blocked (CryptoRank 403, DropsTab 404, Airdrops.io 403), so the calendar sweep leans on ICO Analytics, CoinGabbar, Bitrue and ICO Drops. Those are secondary and partly promotional.
- Founder links and official site and app URLs were mostly not in the pages I could open. Where a field says unknown, the main thread should check the project X account.
- Nothing here is verified against the project's own X or blog. All TGE timing needs a check there before outreach.
