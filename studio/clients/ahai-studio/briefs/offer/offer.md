# Ahai Studio offer

Draft 4, 2026-10-17. An internal clarity document, not a sales page. Every line traces back to `notes.md` in
this folder. Lines marked PROPOSED are not decided yet. The logic chain is untested with buyers.

## Who it is for

Web3 teams with a launch or TGE date 1 to 3 months away, whose brand is weak or missing and whose site and
product still look like the first version their developers built. This is our first segment. The wider market
stays funded tech teams from seed to Series B in SaaS, AI, fintech and web3.

## The offer in one line

Brand, site and one core product flow, designed and coded by one team, live in six weeks, for $15,000 fixed.

## Transformation summary

> We help funded tech teams whose product still looks like the first version their developers built walk into
> their raise or launch looking like the company they have become, through one team that designs and codes the
> brand, the site and the product.

Pains each part answers, from `notes.md`. Discovery answers cost surprise and repeated requirements. Brand and
site answer weak credibility and a brand that no longer fits. The product flow answers the developer built first
version. Tracking answers visitors who do not convert. Support answers nothing after launch. The update cadence
answers passive designers who wait for direction.

## Not in the $15,000

- Copy and positioning. Discovery flags a weak message, and the client brings the words.
- Smart contracts, backend and infrastructure.
- Native mobile apps. We deliver websites and web applications.
- Pages or screens past the limits, quoted before work starts.
- The monthly retainer, $4,000 a month for up to 40 hours, cancelled with one month's notice.

## Timeline

Six weeks from a signed scope to launch. The date holds only if the client gives us access in week 1 and
reviews work within 2 working days. Late feedback moves the launch date by the same number of days.

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Discovery, tracking on the current site, then the scope is signed |
| Week 2 | Brand identity and the launch kit |
| Weeks 3 to 5 | Website and product flow, designed and coded side by side, with a weekly sync |
| Week 6 | Final review, fixes and launch. Tracking goes live on the new site |
| Weeks 7 to 10 | Support, with the day 30 read |

## Price

- $15,000 fixed and published. We never sell hours.
- Half at signing and half at launch.
- The price comes with a case study clause. The client agrees to a written case study with the day 30 numbers. A client who cannot agree, for example under an NDA, pays $20,000.
- Clients we cannot show, such as a Web3 team without a public team, an audit or a working product, pay $20,000 in full up front, in stablecoin or fiat and never in their own token. They never take one of the first 2 to 3 slots, and the studio is not credited on their site.
- The price holds for the first 2 to 3 deals and goes up after 3 client case studies.
- A Brand and Site package at $10,000, components 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6, is a fallback for a team whose product does not need work. It is never offered first.

## After launch

The site goes on the client's own hosting account, which we set up. All intellectual property, meaning the code,
the design files and the brand files, transfers to the client when the second payment lands.

## The argument behind it

The logic chain. Accepted by Filip, untested with buyers. It gets locked after 5 to 10 buyer conversations where
it lands, and before any website copy is written. Testing runs through the teardowns. For Web3 teams the pain
line reads "Our TGE is in a few weeks and our brand and site look like every project that rugged".

| Stage | The argument |
|---|---|
| Pain | We raised, but our product and site still look like the MVP our developers built, and the next raise or launch is when people judge it |
| Step 1 | A first version is built to prove the product works, not to earn trust. Patching it one screen at a time never changes the impression the whole thing makes |
| Step 2 | One consistent version of the company, the brand, the site and the product together, that looks like where the company is now and is live before the date that matters |
| Step 3 | Your developers belong on the roadmap, not rebuilding mockups. So the redesign has to arrive as working code, built by the people who designed it, on a fixed scope and date |
| Conclusion | One team that designs and codes your brand, site and product for a fixed published price, live before your raise or launch, with tracking that shows what changed |

## Why a buyer should believe us

The price, the scope and the launch date are fixed and written down before work starts. The day 30 numbers show
what changed. The same team designs and codes, so nothing is lost between a designer and the client's
developers.

The first three pieces of proof come from teardowns, public redesign concepts of real Web3 sites. Teneo
Protocol goes first, and Strata and Ekiden stay on record. A teardown counts as a success when one founder
replies, or the post brings at least 3 DMs or comments from founders. Teardowns are shown as concept work and do
not count toward the 3 client case studies that raise the price. PROPOSED.
