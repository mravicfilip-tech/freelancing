# Glossary

UI terms for this client. Copy uses these words and no others for the same thing. `/uireview`
checks copy against this file. Add a term the first time the UI needs a new one. The site language
is not settled yet, so terms are in English until Filip names it.

| Term | Means | Never say | Where used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Route | One stretch of trench and duct built for one job, measured in metres or kilometres | Line, path | Projects, numbers band |
| Duct | The empty pipe laid in the trench that the fibre cable is pulled or blown through later | Tube, cable | How a route gets built |

## Verbs

Buttons name what happens. One verb per action across the site.

| Action | Label | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Phone the office | Call us | Contact, Reach out |
| Send the job form | Send the job | Submit, Go |

## Errors

An error says what went wrong and what to do next, in one sentence.
