# Ekotehnika competitive analysis

Site reviewed is ekotehnika.rs, read live on 8 October 2026. Prepared by Filip Mravic.

Language of the site is Serbian (Latin script). Quotes below are translated.

---

## 1. Summary in five lines

- Ekotehnika presents itself as the official Linde Material Handling dealer for Serbia and Montenegro. It is based in Vrčin on the south edge of Belgrade.
- The site sells, rents, services and supplies parts for Linde forklifts and warehouse trucks, and pitches Linde safety tech and automation.
- It is a B2B lead generation site. Nothing is bought online. Every path ends in a quote request, a phone call or an email.
- The real business is the service relationship. The site calls the service centre "the heart of our company" and sells new and used units, rental and contracts around it.
- The site is mostly Linde's own dealer template and copy, with a thin local layer. Local proof, prices, stock and fresh content are weak or missing.

## 3. What the site is for

- Capture inbound leads from companies that move pallets and goods, and route them to sales, service or accounting.
- Act as the Serbian storefront for the full Linde catalogue. The product finder lists 96 new models and 84 rental models.
- Rank in Serbian search for forklift price, rental and buying guide terms through a blog.
- Signal legitimacy as the official Linde partner, which matters because the same brand is also sold used by grey importers.

It is not an e-commerce site. There is no cart, no online booking, no customer portal and no live price list.

## 5. Business angle

**Model.** A franchised OEM dealer. Ekotehnika earns margin on new units, but the dependable money is recurring service, parts, rental and service contracts on the installed base. The site says this out loud by calling service the heart of the firm.

**Moat.** Official Linde status since 2023, which it describes as general agent for Serbia and Montenegro. Exclusivity is self reported and not checked against Linde's own dealer list. The status gives it access to Linde factory training, original parts, the Approved Trucks used program and Linde's rental pool, which competitors selling used Linde trucks cannot match.

**Origin story matters.** Ekotehnika grew up as a used Linde importer and repairer for 25 years, then took over the official dealership. Its instinct is service and used units, not enterprise solution selling. The automation and fleet pages read as Linde corporate copy, not as something the local team actively sells yet.

**Positioning.** Premium brand, safety and total cost of ownership rather than lowest purchase price. Their own price article concedes Linde, Toyota and Jungheinrich cost 20% to 30% more and argues it pays back over 5 to 7 years of heavy use.

**Funnel shape.**
1. Search traffic lands on a blog post or a product page.
2. The visitor filters the finder or reads the buying guide.
3. A quote request, phone call or email goes to sales or service.
4. A salesperson qualifies and quotes by hand.
5. The win turns into a service or full service contract, which is where lifetime value sits.

## 6. User personas

These personas are inferred from what the site sells and writes about. They are not based on customer data or interviews.

### Persona A. Warehouse and logistics manager at a distribution centre

| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Runs a 3PL, retail DC or wholesale warehouse with a multi shift truck fleet |
| Wants | Uptime, fast repair, the right truck for narrow aisles and high racks, fewer accidents |
| Pains | Downtime from breakdowns, mixed fleet from several brands, peak season capacity, safety incidents and inspections |
| What the site gives them | Reach trucks, narrow aisle trucks, order pickers, safety tech, full service contracts, rental for peaks |
| What is missing | Named response times, local references, any proof from Serbian warehouses |

### Persona B. Plant or production manager in manufacturing

| | |
|---|---|

| Wants | Heavy capacity trucks, attachments for paper rolls or timber, compliance with group HSE rules, possible automation |
| Pains | Safety audits from HQ, labour shortage, cost per pallet move |
| What the site gives them | Heavy trucks up to 18 t, industry blog posts on paper, timber and agriculture, AGV pages, Zero Accident safety suite |
| What is missing | Case studies from Serbia, an automation consultation offer with a named person, ROI figures |

| | |
|---|---|

## 8. Site structure and experience

**Navigation.** Eleven top level items. New, Used, Rental, Service, Parts, Automation, Safety, Industrial robots, Blog, About, Contact. Several dropdown headers link to "#" and go nowhere.

**Homepage order.** Promo hero for the MT15 C pallet truck at 1,390 EUR, three quick links (rental, new trucks, fleet optimisation), three Linde value cards (productivity, safety, ergonomics), two content cards, blog teasers.

**Conversion points.**
- "Request a quote" and "Rent this truck" on every product page.
- A "What can we do for you?" selector with only two options, new vehicles or rental vehicles.
- Contact form hosted on linde-mh.rs, not on the dealer's own domain.
- Department email buttons for sales, service and accounting.

**Defects and inconsistencies seen on the day.**
- Used "in stock" page shows 0 products, checked twice on 8 October 2026, while the menu promotes it.
- The homepage hero shows a 1,390 EUR price, but the MT15 C product page shows no price and no promo terms.
- Two homepage cards share the title "Smart and flexible, automation for intralogistics", while the second card's text is about the Approved Trucks used program.
- The fleet management page is about driverless AGVs, not telematics or fleet management.
- About page claims "more than 100 years" of experience, which is Linde's history, alongside "almost 30 years", which is Ekotehnika's.
- Five different phone numbers appear across pages, with different labels for the same number.
- No opening hours, no map embed, no named contacts on the contact page.
- Blog has not been updated since 19 December 2025, almost ten months before this review.
- The selector form offers no path for service, parts or used trucks, even though these are core revenue lines.

**Look and feel.** Clean, Linde branded, red and grey, card based. Credible but generic. It reads as Linde with a local phone number.

## 9. Content and search

- Blog ran at roughly three posts a week through late 2025, then stopped. The archive goes back to March 2025 and spans 12 pages.
- Topics are buying intent and industry specific. New forklift price, push pull attachments, timber, paper rolls, agriculture, slip sheets, cross docking, women operators.
- The price article is the strongest lead magnet. It gives EUR ranges (electric 1.5 to 2 t at 20,000 to 25,000 EUR, diesel 2.5 to 3 t at 20,000 to 30,000 EUR, heavy trucks 40,000 to 70,000 EUR) and a new vs used rule of thumb.
- Page titles target "Ovlašćeni zastupnik Linde viljuškara u Srbiji" (authorised Linde forklift dealer in Serbia) and generic forklift terms.
- Most service, safety and automation content is Linde's localised corporate copy, so it carries little unique search value.

## 10. Competitive landscape in Serbia

| Competitor | Model | Strength against Ekotehnika | Weakness against Ekotehnika |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jungheinrich Srbija | Manufacturer owned subsidiary since 1990, based in Novi Banovci | Direct OEM presence, reported 27% turnover growth in Serbia in 2023 | Premium price, same tier as Linde |
| Vecar Viljuškari | Authorised CESAB dealer (Toyota group) since 2007, multi brand used | Five service points (Belgrade, Novi Sad, Bačka Palanka, Vršac, Kragujevac), sells many brands | Not an official Linde, Toyota or Still dealer |
| PC Panex (Novi Sad) | Multi brand used importer from Germany and the Netherlands, service, replacement trucks | Lower prices, sells used Linde too, 25 years in market | No OEM backing, no factory warranty program |
| Formis plus and small service shops | Independent repair and rental | Price, flexibility, local relationships | No OEM parts or training |
| Marketplaces (polovniautomobili.com, Supralift) | Used listings | Where SME buyers actually shop for used trucks | Not a service partner |

**Where Ekotehnika sits.** By its own account the only official Linde channel in the country, with service roots. It has one address in Vrčin and claims field vans across Serbia, against Vecar's five listed service points and Jungheinrich's direct OEM operation. Its own site is weaker than its market position.

## 11. Strengths

- Official Linde status, self reported as exclusive, with access to Linde's full range, rental pool, Approved Trucks and parts.
- Service heritage of nearly 30 years and a claimed national field coverage.
- Broad offer under one roof. New, used, rental, service, parts, safety, automation.
- Rental terms are customer friendly. Hours to a year, 24 hour delivery, free transport, swaps allowed.
- A buying guide with real EUR price ranges, rare in this market.
- A strong safety story borrowed from Linde.

## 12. Weaknesses and gaps

- No local proof. No Serbian customer logos, case studies, testimonials or project photos.
- No prices beyond one hero promo. Rental, contracts and used units are all "ask us".
- Used stock empty on the day of review, while this is the segment SME buyers want most.
- No financing or leasing offer, though the About page lists financing.
- No service promise in numbers. "Short response times" and "24/7" with no hours or SLA tiers.
- Lead capture leaks to linde-mh.rs, and the selector ignores service, parts and used trucks.
- Stale content. No blog post since December 2025.
- Copy errors and mismatched pages lower trust for a buyer comparing three suppliers.
- One address, while the main local rival lists five service points. Ekotehnika leans on field vans instead and gives no numbers for them.

## 13. Opportunities

Purpose of this brief, Filip to add. Until then the openings below are written for anyone competing with Ekotehnika or advising it.

- **Publish the service promise.** Response time in hours by region, contract tiers, number of technicians and vans. This is the core business and it has no numbers.
- **Show local proof.** Three to five Serbian case studies by industry, matching the blog topics already written (timber, paper, agriculture, cross dock).
- **Price transparency.** "From" prices on rental per day and per month, sample service contract rates, used prices on every listing.
- **Fill and feed the used stock.** Connect real stock, add the Approved Trucks warranty to each card, and push the same feed to polovniautomobili.com.
- **Own the lead form.** Host it on ekotehnika.rs with options for service, parts, used and rental, plus a callback request.
- **Add a finance path.** Leasing partner, monthly payment indications on new units.
- **Safety retrofit offer.** A packaged retrofit for mixed brand fleets, aimed at HSE managers, priced per truck.
- **Restart content.** Keep the buying guide format, which is the strongest asset, and add TCO calculators for electric vs diesel and new vs used.
