How it runs

Scroll to arrive, a clip shows the service

Each service has three beats. Beat 1 arrives with scroll. Beat 2 is a clip of about 3 seconds that plays by itself once you stop, so the visitor watches instead of working the wheel. Beat 3 lands with scroll. Scrolling during a clip skips it to its end, scrolling back plays everything in reverse, and nothing ever holds the wheel.

Dark blocks are the clips. The service rail is live from the first screen, so someone who only needs Servis clicks it and lands on S1 without passing the other three.

Product thinking

What a visitor must be able to say after each service

The visitor is a warehouse or logistics manager, a plant manager or a small business owner with one or two trucks. Nothing is sold online, so each service ends on one call to action, a quote or a call. The test is one sentence a stranger repeats from the scene alone.

Najam

I can get a forklift within a day for my busy weeks, and they take it back when I am done.

Polovni

A used Linde comes back renewed, with a real warranty, for less than new.

Novi

Whatever my hall looks like, there is a Linde model built for it.

Servis

When my forklift stops, a van comes to me and gets it running again.

Rules every screen follows

UX and brand rules

  • The first screen names all four services and shows the quote button and the sales phone, readable in five seconds, as the brief asks.
  • Red is for primary actions only, the quote button and the call to action in the panel. Everything else is ink and grey. Trucks look like real Linde trucks, red only on the rear shell.
  • The panel holds a headline, at most four short bullets, and one image, model card or number. Facts are only the company's own claims.
  • The call to action for the service stays at the bottom of the panel for all three beats, so it is always one click away.
  • A skip link, Preskoči animaciju, jumps past the hero for keyboard and screen reader users.
  • With reduced motion there is no pin and no clip. The four services show as four still panels, beat 3 of each, so nobody loses three of the four.
  • No made up tools. The quote goes to the contact page, never a fake configurator.

UI, three directions times three versions

Nine combinations from one story

The story, the wireframe and the screens above stay the same in all nine. Versions 2 Linija, 4 Grad and 5 Sistem keep their own scene and motion, and each gets direction A, B and C. The panel and nav are built once as three themes, so the nine stay in step.

A. Soft sheet

Large white panel on a pale ground

A very rounded white panel, big light weight numbers with a small unit after a slash, hairline square icon buttons, and model cards with a four column spec row of real figures. The closing screen borrows the warehouse dashboard's vehicle cards with a status dot and a progress line.

B. Glow cards

Ink panel, lit from inside

The panel is ink with a soft grey and white glow, white type, dot rows for steps like the seven renewal stations, a row of round icon stats, list rows with an arrow, and light streaks on the fast moments, the delivery truck and the service van.

C. Pills and dots

Frosted panel, a very large headline

A very large tight headline per service, a black active pill on the rail, the red call to action as a pill ending in a round arrow, small dark status chips with a live dot, and the Servis map inside a dark rounded card.

Before building

Open questions, each with a proposed default

Reply with yes, or correct one line.

  1. Length. 9,000px with a clip in the middle of each service. Your 12,000px works if the clips feel rushed, every number here scales by a third.

    Default, 9,000px.

  2. Order. Najam, Polovni, Novi, Servis, as you said it. Servis is the core of the business and comes last, so the rail does the work for service customers.

    Default, keep your order.

  3. Build order. Build the story in 2 Linija first with UI A, B and C, you pick, then port the story to 4 and 5.

    Default, yes.

  4. The rail. Your wireframe keeps the four service bar along the bottom and the pill nav at the top links to the site pages.

    Default, keep both, the bottom bar becomes a slim rail that jumps between services.

  5. The seven renewal steps. Ekotehnika says Approved Trucks are renewed in seven steps but does not name them.

    Default, seven numbered stations with no names until Ekotehnika gives them.

  6. Revora type. Its condensed italic capitals break the Geist ruling from 8 October.

    Default, keep Geist.

  7. New copy. Problem and outcome lines are new, facts are the company's own.

    Default, you correct the Serbian before the build.

Facts come from the hero brief, the competitive analysis and the MT15 C product page in the client folder. No people, testimonials, rental prices or invented numbers.
