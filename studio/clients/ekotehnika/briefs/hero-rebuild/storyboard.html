How it runs

Scroll to arrive, a clip shows the service

Each service has three beats. Beat 1 arrives with scroll. Beat 2 is a clip of about 3 seconds that plays by itself once the visitor stops, so they watch instead of working the wheel. Beat 3 lands with scroll. Scrolling during a clip skips it to its end, scrolling back plays everything in reverse, and nothing ever holds the wheel. When the service changes, the panel content swaps, the old lines lift out and the new ones rise in, about 0.45 s.

Dark blocks are the clips. The rail under the scene is live from the first screen, so someone who only needs Servis clicks it and lands on S1.

Rules every keyframe follows

UX and brand rules

  • The first screen names all four services and shows the quote button and the sales phone, readable in five seconds, as the brief asks.
  • Red is for primary actions only, the quote button and the call to action in the panel. Everything else is ink and grey. Trucks look like real Linde trucks, red only on the rear shell.
  • The panel holds a headline, at most four short bullets, and one number, model card or image. Facts are only the company's own claims.
  • The call to action for the service stays at the bottom of the panel for all three beats.
  • A skip link, Preskoči animaciju, jumps past the hero. With reduced motion there is no pin and no clip, the four services show as four still panels.

UI, three directions times three versions

Nine combinations from one story

The story, the wireframe and the keyframes stay the same in all nine. Versions 2 Linija, 4 Grad and 5 Sistem keep their own scene and motion, and each gets direction A, B and C. The panel and nav are built once as three themes.

A. Soft sheet

Large white panel on a pale ground

A very rounded white panel, big light weight numbers with a small unit after a slash, hairline square icon buttons, model cards with a four column spec row of real figures, and vehicle cards with a status dot on the closing screen.

B. Glow cards

Ink panel, lit from inside

The panel is ink with a soft grey and white glow, white type, dot rows for steps like the seven renewal stations, round icon stats, list rows with an arrow, and light streaks on the fast moments.

C. Pills and dots

Frosted panel, a very large headline

A very large tight headline per service, a black active pill on the rail, the red call to action as a pill ending in a round arrow, small dark status chips with a live dot, and the Servis map in a dark rounded card.

Before building

Open questions, each with a proposed default

Reply with yes, or correct one line.

  1. Length. 9,000px with a clip in the middle of each service. 12,000px works if the clips feel rushed, every number here scales by a third.

    Default, 9,000px.

  2. Order. Najam, Polovni, Novi, Servis, as you said it. Servis is the core of the business and comes last, so the rail does the work for service customers.

    Default, keep your order.

  3. Build order. Build the story in 2 Linija first with UI A, B and C, you pick, then port the story to 4 and 5.

    Default, yes.

  4. The rail. The bottom bar becomes a slim rail that jumps between services, the pill nav at the top links to the site pages.

    Default, keep both.

  5. The seven renewal steps. Ekotehnika says Approved Trucks are renewed in seven steps but does not name them.

    Default, seven numbered stations with no names until Ekotehnika gives them.

  6. Revora type. Its condensed italic capitals break the Geist ruling from 8 October.

    Default, keep Geist.

  7. New copy. Problem and outcome lines are new, facts are the company's own.

    Default, you correct the Serbian before the build.

Facts come from the hero brief, the competitive analysis and the MT15 C product page in the client folder. No people, testimonials, rental prices or invented numbers.
