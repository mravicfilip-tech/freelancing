## Four pages, in the shop's frame, at /najam, /servis, /delovi and /kompanija

Each is built of the shop's own parts, the grid of four frames, the collage hero, cells with icons and ticks, the figures row, and ends
on its own form topic. What makes each one interesting is a part that moves or answers, not decoration.

1. Najam. The hero with the client's line, od nekoliko sati do godinu dana, isporuka za 24 sata, over a collage of the ten delivery
   photos. The four facts, 24 sata isporuka, besplatna dostava i preuzimanje, održavanje uključeno, zamena viljuškara. Two ways to rent
   side by side, kratkoročno from a day to a year for seasonal peaks and to try before buying, and dugoročno with a picker for 36, 48, 60
   and 72 months, the client's own ask. Then the trucks to rent, the shop's categories, each to Pitajte za najam. Ends on the form with
   Najam picked.
2. Servis. The hero with Hitan servis and its number at full size and Zakažite servis. The servis map from the homepage, Vrčin and Čurug
   with their roads across Serbia, the two addresses and phones beside it. Five services as cells, redovno održavanje, hitne
   intervencije, zakonski pregledi, ugovor o punom servisu, popravka više od 300 tipova vozila i drugih marki. The pre-operation check as
   five tabs, konstrukcija i vozni mehanizam, motor i baterija, hidraulika, elektrika, sistem za prihvat tereta. Photos from the
   company's own service posts. Ends on the form with Servis picked.
3. Delovi. The hero, originalni Linde delovi. A grid of the part groups the Drive has pictures for, potrošni delovi, baterije i punjači,
   sedišta, servisni setovi, sprejevi, komande i držači, each to the form with that group filled in. How to order in three steps,
   model i serijski broj, deo ili fotografija, ponuda. Ends on the form with a new topic, Delovi.
4. Kompanija. The hero over a drone photo of Vrčin, Snaga koja pokreće, od 1997. širom Srbije i Crne Gore. The figures, 1997, 1.000+
   klijenata, 2 servisna centra, 300+ tipova vozila. The two sites with their addresses and the map. Work at clients' sites, Belt and
   Vemax photos. Linde events, LINDE HUB and Stapler Cup. Ends on Kontakt.

The menu's Usluge panel and Kompanija link point to these pages instead of the homepage chapters.
