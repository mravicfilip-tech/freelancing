# Linde brand rules for the Ekotehnika dealer website

Sources are the LMH Co-Branding Guideline 1.3 (EN, 19.12.2022, 119 pages, cited as "G p.N") and the Ekotehnika Marketing Playbook pptx (3 slides, cited as "PB s.N"). The guideline has no website chapter. Web rules below are carried over from the screen, online media and logo pages. Anything inferred is marked **uncertain**.

## 1. Logo and dealer logo

- The LMH logo may not be altered. Dealer logos may only appear next to the LMH logo (G p.5).
- Two lockups exist. Usage 1 is the layout principle with a grey field. Usage 2 is outside the layout principle (G p.5).
- Usage 1 is for media where the LMH brand is strong, and it "works well for large online banners". On very small formats use Usage 2 (G p.6).
- Usage 1 puts the dealer logo under the LMH logo on LMH pale grey `#e9e9e9`. The field is as wide as the LMH logo and 3/4 as high. The dealer logo is centred with at least 2x on every side (G p.6).
- x is 1/16 of the LMH logo width (G p.6, p.11).
- The dealer logo may never be more prominent than the LMH logo (G p.6, p.8).
- Usage 2 option 1 puts the logos side by side. The dealer logo is exactly as wide as the LMH logo, centred on its height, at least 1x above and below it, and about 3x apart (G p.8, p.62).
- Usage 2 option 2 stacks them. The dealer logo is centred under the LMH logo with 1x left and right (G p.8).
- The LMH logo ratio is always 5 to 3. Smallest screen size is 160 by 96px (G p.11). The e-mail signature uses exactly 160 by 96px (G p.32).
- Clear space outside the layout principle is 3x, the distance between the lettering and the logo field edge. At 160px wide, x is 10px, so 30px (G p.11).
- Dealer logo colour is monochrome LMH graphite `#4d4d4d`. No RGB logos, no other colours, no extra slogans or graphics beside it (G p.6, p.15 note 2).
- A dealer may add a claim, a brand mark or an extra logo colour only in Usage 2 and only "if necessary". Sale logos are to be omitted (G p.10). **Uncertain** whether this extension applies on screen, it is shown on print items.
- White dealer logo is allowed in video only, to the right of the LMH logo in Usage 2 (G p.57 to p.58). For a website, graphite on white or pale grey is the safe reading. **Uncertain** whether white on a dark web ground would be approved, ask the LMH marketing contact.
- Forbidden for the LMH logo (G p.12)
  - on a red surface
  - on a darker grey or any undefined colour
  - directly on photos (it needs the white passepartout or a white field)
  - with an added border
  - with rearranged elements
  - with changed proportions
- Allowed grounds are white, LMH pale grey, and the layout principle over a cover image or a design colour (G p.12).
- No boxing, effects, shadows or recolouring of either logo. Shadow and recolour are not named line by line, they follow from "may not be altered" (G p.5). **Uncertain** wording.
- Use the supplied logo files. Specific print and screen files sit on the LMH Xtranet, not in this PDF (G p.5).

- Information colours also come in 72% and 48% tints. Forklift red 72% `#e5684b`, 48% `#eca287`. Blue 72% `#468cc4`, 48% `#8fb0d9`. Turquoise 72% `#7fbccd`, 48% `#b0d2de`. Green 72% `#a4c778`, 48% `#c6dba9`. Yellow 72% `#ffdb70`, 48% `#ffe7a6`.
- LMH silver is print only and is not for screens (G p.15).
- LMH red may only be used on white and LMH pale grey, and this also applies to the logo field. No red on dark grounds (G p.14).
- Use "lots of white" and sparing grey design colours. Red is for outlining and emphasis. Text and lines should be black (G p.14).
- Pale grey is only for background fields. The other greys are used less often (G p.14).
- Information colours are only for content such as images, graphs, tables and diagrams, and only at 72% and 48% tints (G p.14).
- Buttons are LMH red with white text on a pale grey background (newsletter rule, G p.63). **Uncertain** for the website, but it matches the live site.
- No percentage cap on red is given. Only "lots of white" and "sparing" grey.
- The guideline does not cover colour on dark grounds beyond the bans on logo and red. Dark grounds are not endorsed anywhere in print or online pages. Video uses white text on a red band and on dark grey (G p.58 to p.59).
- **Mismatch with client tokens.** The guideline red is `#A00020`. The live site and README use `#aa0020`, plus `#990e1f`, `#cc132a`, `#94001d`, `#700016` and `#ba1926`. None of those extra reds are in the LMH palette. README says not to invent colours, so the site tokens stay, but `#A00020` is the official value. Text grey `#4a595c` and ink `#222222` are also not LMH values (the guideline says black). Filip decides whether to align them.

## 3. Typography

- House font is FF Dax Pro (Medium, Regular, Light), a narrow sans serif by Hans Reichel (G p.13).
- For digital body text in small sizes use FF Daxline Pro Regular (G p.13 note 2).
- Fallback if the house font cannot be used is Arial (G p.13). Newsletters, e-mail signatures and video subtitles use Arial (G p.32, p.59, p.63). Fira Sans on the live site is not an approved fallback. **Uncertain** whether that matters to LMH.
- Licence terms for installing the font are on the Xtranet and are not in this PDF (G p.13 note 1). The client README already says never download or embed Dax.
- Weights. Headlines in Medium, body in Regular, Light as a third cut (G p.13, p.58).
- Case. Video titles and names are Dax Medium all caps (G p.58 to p.59). Print ad headlines are shown in all caps (G p.45). Body text is mixed case. The "text hierarchy" with case rules for layouts is not shown on the pages read, so this is **uncertain** for web headings.
- Headline writing (G p.45) is benefit first, short, active, customer focused. The offer goes in the subline. Use as little punctuation as possible, no commas or dashes in headlines, a full stop only on a full sentence.
- Sizes. No general web scale exists. The only screen sizes given are newsletter headlines 18px and 22px, newsletter price 20px, e-mail text 10pt, video text 90 to 120px at 1920 by 1080 (G p.32, p.58, p.62).

## 4. Layout principle

- All printed media use a white passepartout (frame) around the image with the LMH logo top right in a white border, sitting on the image area (G p.7).
- The passepartout is 6mm on A4 and the logo is 49.5 by 29.7mm. The grid is 12 columns on A4 with a centre bar of 4mm (G p.7).
- The frame around the logo is 2/3 of the passepartout width (G p.6).
- Online the principle is kept for the large billboard banner of 850 by 250px (G p.56). Skyscraper, super banner and full banner skip it and only put the dealer logo on a pale grey field.
- For a web page this suggests a white frame, logo top right on a white field, and a pale grey dealer cell. **Uncertain**, it is an inference and not a stated web rule.
- The claim "IMMER AN IHRER SEITE" in German, "ALWAYS ON YOUR SIDE" in English, appears in the Richter Fördertechnik example on brochure covers (G p.6, p.7, p.37, p.38). It is shown as the title on a sample, not set as a mandatory element. The guideline gives no usage rule for it. **Uncertain** whether it can be used on the Ekotehnika site, ask LMH before using it. A Serbian line would need LMH approval.
- Image copy must stay readable. Lighten the motif or move the headline to a lighter area (G p.44).

## 5. Imagery

- Four categories (G p.17). Image is assertive and visionary. Product is innovative and genuine. Service and people is close and professional. Documentary is efficient and robust.
- Light is bright and warm (G p.18).
- Perspective is frontal with fewer sharp lines, or a natural documentary angle (G p.18).
- Colour is reduced in saturation, with the brand red accentuated (G p.18). Natural skin tones (G p.59).
- Composition puts people at the centre. Mix close-ups with long lens shots. Shallow depth of field for people, wide angle with deep depth of field is also allowed (G p.18).
- Content is real products in real application scenes. Documentary "does not give the impression of being staged" (G p.18).
- The pages show real photos of trucks in warehouses, red trucks on grey concrete, with real staff. No illustrations, renders or gradient art appear in the photo section. The guideline does not rule on renders. **Uncertain**.
- Backgrounds in the samples are warehouse, racking and bright halls. Photos in the guideline are style references only (G p.16).
- Pictograms (G p.19 to p.20) are drawn on a 32px grid with a 2px line. Use them in red on white or pale grey, white on red, or black or dark grey on white or pale grey. They are RGB downloads on the Xtranet.
- Social posts should avoid heavy text on images (G p.61).

## 6. Online media pages

- Web banners (G p.56). Billboard 850 by 250, rectangle 300 by 250, super banner 827 by 90, full banner 468 by 60, skyscraper 160 by 600. Text may sit on a transparent ground. On a light page a thin line borders the banner. Content may animate and logos stay static.
- Video (G p.57 to p.59). Logo top left because platforms put controls top right. LMH logo 380px wide on 1920 by 1080, dealer logo white beside it in Usage 2. Title is Dax Medium all caps in white on a red first level band and black or white on a second level. Intro shows for at least 2 seconds, logo fades in on the outro. Natural colours and subtle motion only.
- Social (G p.60 to p.61). Dealers who mostly sell Linde may use the full co-branding in a square profile picture, and circular crops use the dealer logo only. Do not embed a logo in the cover image. No price ranges such as euro signs. Legal information must be reachable within two clicks.
- Newsletter (G p.62 to p.63). Arial, headlines 18px and 22px, logos in Usage 2 at 3x apart, background pale grey, red buttons with white text. At least two thirds of the content must relate to LMH.
- E-mail banner (G p.33). No co-branding. A 600 by 160px display with a 2px light grey `#d2d2d2` border and an arrow pictogram button.

## 7. Marketing Playbook for dealers

- It is Ekotehnika's own short playbook with 3 slides, and it is in Serbian (PB s.1).
- It names the company "Ekotehnika Viljuškari doo Vrčin, ekskluzivni distributer Linde MH za Srbiju i Crnu Goru" (the source spells it "Ekluzivni"). This is the dealer claim to use. The brief calls Ekotehnika an official dealer, the playbook says exclusive distributor, so confirm the wording.
- Reels and stories (PB s.2). Font Dax Pro, glow shadow effect and no outline, logo top left.
- Feed posts (PB s.3). No font on the images and no logo on the images. The message goes in the caption with hashtags.
- There are no tone of voice rules, no approved claims and no messaging pillars in the playbook. Anything on tone is **uncertain** and comes only from G p.45 headline rules.
- The playbook is for social. It says nothing about the website.
